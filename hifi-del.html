PREPARATION OF A DELETION MUTANT — HIFI NEBUILDER

1.
Retrieve the gene sequence from the PomBase database and import it into SnapGene.
Add approximately ±1000 bp of flanking sequence so that the surrounding genomic region is visible.
Annotate the gene, including the ORF and UTRs, in the sequence.
2.
Inspect the gene in its genomic context to determine whether it overlaps with any other genes.
Annotate any overlapping genes in SnapGene.
Modify the deletion design as appropriate if an overlap is identified.
3.
Select the appropriate plasmid.
Consider the experiments planned for the deletion mutant in advance when choosing the plasmid.
4.
Select suitable restriction endonucleases. Examples:
  • pHyg → HindIII, SmaI, BglII, BamHI
  • pKan → BglII, SmaI, BamHI
  • pNAT → HindIII, BglII, BamHI
5.
Proceed with the construct design in NEBuilder.
6.
Fragment 1: plasmid sequence. Process the plasmid sequence as a circular vector, name the fragment, and select the restriction endonucleases required to generate the linearized fragment.
Alternatively, the vector can be prepared by inverse PCR.
7.
Fragment 2: upstream (UP) homologous sequence. Select the appropriate upstream homologous region, process the sequence, and generate its complement.
8.
Fragment 3: downstream (DOWN) homologous sequence. Process the sequence and generate its complement.
9.
Edit one of the UP/DOWN fragments and introduce a linearization site between them.
Important: The endonuclease recognition sequence used for linearization of the final plasmid must be unique.
Examples include ApaI (GGGCCC) or XbaI (TCTAGA).
10.
Run the assembly reaction in SnapGene.
