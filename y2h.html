Y2H

Yeast two-hybrid

Principle

The yeast two-hybrid (Y2H) system is used to detect protein–protein interactions. The system is based on two separate domains of a transcription factor: the DNA-binding domain (BD) and the activation domain (AD).

One protein of interest is fused to the BD, while the second protein is fused to the AD. If the two proteins interact, the BD and AD are brought together, allowing transcription of reporter genes and detection of the interaction.

Yeast Transformation

  1. Inoculate a fresh yeast strain into 10 mL YPD and cultivate O/N at 28–30 °C with shaking.
  2. In the morning, refresh the culture into 100 mL YPD, OD600 = 0.2.
    • Vmin = 2 mL (1 transformation)
  3. Cultivate at 28–30 °C with shaking until OD600 = 0.5–1.
  4. Prepare pDNA (250–500 ng) in 1.5 mL Eppendorf microtubes.
  5. Prepare solutions for 10 mL:
    • TE → 1 mL 10× TE + 9 mL milliQ dH2O
    • TE + LiAc → 1 mL 10× TE + 1 mL LiAc 1 M + 8 mL milliQ dH2O
    • TE + LiAc + PEG → 1 mL 10× TE + 1 mL LiAc 1 M + 8 mL PEG 50% (mix well)
  6. Centrifuge cells in 50 mL Falcon tubes for 5 min at 2600 rpm, RT.
    • Centrifuge the cells needed for transformation (2 mL per transformation).
  7. Remove the supernatant and wash the cells in 10 mL milliQ dH2O. Centrifuge for 5 min at 2600 rpm, RT.
  8. Remove the supernatant, resuspend the pellet in 1 mL TE + LiAc, and vortex briefly.
  9. Transfer the suspension into 2 mL Eppendorf microtubes and centrifuge for 3 min at 5000 rpm, RT.
  10. Remove the supernatant and resuspend the pellet in TE + LiAc + volume according to the number of transformations.
    • 50 µL per transformation
  11. Aliquot 50 µL of cell suspension into microtubes containing the prepared DNA.
  12. Add 300 µL of TE + LiAc + PEG and mix well/vortex.
  13. Incubate the transformations for 30 min at 30 °C with shaking.
  14. Heat shock for 15 min at 42 °C with vigorous shaking.
    • PEG sediments.
  15. Short spin for 5 s (not more!) and remove the supernatant.
  16. Gently resuspend the cells in 50 µL TE.
  17. Plate 25 µL of the transformation reaction onto an SD-LW plate (2 transformations per plate are OK).
  • To be safe, store the rest of the transformation at 4 °C if it needs to be replicated.
  • Do not forget to transform single proteins with the opposite empty vector domain (e.g. Rad51-BD + EV-AD / Rad51-AD + EV-BD).

Spot Test

  1. After transformation, replate grown colonies on SD-LW (2 days growth).
  2. Inoculate colonies into 4 mL YPD and cultivate O/N at 28–30 °C with shaking.
  3. Count cells in a Bürker chamber.
  4. Spot cells in 5×10 dilutions, from a maximum of 105 cells to a minimum of 101 cells, on SD-LW, SD-LWH, and SD-LWA plates. Consider using 3-AT; its use is recommended.
  5. Grow spots for 3–4 days at 28–30 °C.

3-AT

3-AT is used in Y2H to reduce background growth caused by basal activation of the HIS3 reporter. It can be added to the selection medium to increase the stringency of the interaction assay.

3-AT 0.25 mM; 0.5 mM; 1 mM; 5 mM; 10 mM; 20 mM
