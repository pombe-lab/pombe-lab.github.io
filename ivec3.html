iVEC3 cloning

in vivo E. coli cloning

Principle

The iVEC system uses the endogenous DNA-repair and replication machinery of Escherichia coli to assemble linear DNA fragments carrying homologous overlapping sequences after they enter the cell. Unlike Gibson Assembly or HiFi DNA Assembly, which assemble fragments in vitro using an added enzyme mix, iVEC assembly occurs inside the bacterial cell.

The key exonuclease direction differs: iVEC relies mainly on E. coli Exonuclease III (XthA), a 3′→5′ exonuclease, which resects the 3′ ends and exposes complementary 5′ single-stranded overhangs. Gibson/HiFi assembly commonly uses T5 exonuclease, which resects the 5′→3′ direction to expose complementary 3′ overhangs. The complementary overlaps anneal; the cell then processes and repairs the assembled DNA.

  2. If the vector was prepared by inverse PCR:
    • Add 0.5 µL DpnI and incubate for 15 min at 37 °C.
    • The vector can alternatively be prepared by restriction digestion.
  3. Purify vector(s) and insert(s) using a purification kit.
  4. Measure sample concentrations.

Why add DpnI? DpnI cuts methylated DNA at its recognition site, 5′-Gm6ATC-3′. Plasmids propagated in standard E. coli strains are generally Dam-methylated, whereas newly synthesized PCR products are unmethylated and therefore resistant to DpnI. Digesting the reaction reduces carry-over of the parental template plasmid and can lower background colonies after transformation.

iVEC3 system transformation

  1. Thaw frozen competent cells on ice.
  2. Add vector and insert, mixing gently by pipetting.
  3. Incubate on ice for 20 min.
  4. Add 1 mL room-temperature LB broth and mix by inversion (no heat shock required).
  5. Incubate in a 37 °C water bath for 45 min, without shaking.
  6. Centrifuge at 5,000 × g for 1 min at room temperature.
  7. Discard the supernatant, leaving approximately 100 µL, and resuspend the whole cell pellet.
  8. Spread the entire volume onto an agar plate and incubate overnight at 37 °C.

Control: Always include a linearized-vector-only control.
